Window Replacement prices in New York

New York State costs are dominated by the NYC metro area, where labor, insurance, and permitting push quotes well above national averages. Upstate markets run meaningfully lower but still above the national midpoint. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.25x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside New York can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • Frame material (vinyl is most affordable; wood and fiberglass cost more)
  • Window type and size (double-hung, casement, bay, picture, custom shapes)
  • Glass package (double vs triple pane, low-E coatings, gas fills)
  • Number of windows (per-window labor drops on whole-house jobs)
  • Structural repairs to sills, frames, or rotten trim discovered during removal

What to know before you get quotes

Window replacement is priced per window installed, averaging $450 to $1,500, with the unit itself ($250 to $1,400 or more) making up 60% to 70% of that figure. Frame material sets the baseline: vinyl is the value leader at $200 to $1,500 per window installed, fiberglass runs $400 to $2,000, and premium wood costs the most while aluminum sits at the budget end but insulates poorly. Quantity changes the math in the homeowner's favor: installers discount per-window labor on whole-house projects, so replacing 15 windows at once costs notably less per window than replacing three. The glass package is the other lever, with triple-pane and specialty low-E coatings adding cost up front in exchange for energy savings, a tradeoff that pays best in very hot or cold climates. Expect quotes to shift if the crew finds rot or out-of-square openings during removal; a good contract names the allowance for repairs before work starts. Always compare quotes on identical specs: same frame material, glass package, grid style, and warranty terms.

FAQ

How much does it cost to replace one window?

A single standard window costs $450 to $1,500 installed, with vinyl double-hung windows at the low end and large custom wood windows at the high end.

Is it cheaper to replace all windows at once?

Yes. Whole-house jobs of 10 to 15 windows typically cost less per window because crews price labor more efficiently on one visit, and suppliers discount bulk orders.

How long do replacement windows last?

Quality vinyl and fiberglass windows last 20 to 40 years; wood windows last 30+ years with maintenance. Most manufacturers warranty the glass seal for 10 to 20 years.
