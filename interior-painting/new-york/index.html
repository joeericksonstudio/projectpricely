Interior Painting prices in New York

New York State costs are dominated by the NYC metro area, where labor, insurance, and permitting push quotes well above national averages. Upstate markets run meaningfully lower but still above the national midpoint. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.25x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside New York can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • Wall condition and prep work (patching, sanding, priming)
  • Paint quality tier and brand
  • Ceilings, trim, and doors included or excluded
  • Number of coats and color changes (dark to light needs more coats)
  • Room height and detail work (stairwells, crown molding)

What to know before you get quotes

Interior painting is priced by paintable surface area, not floor area, which is the single most common source of estimate confusion. A rough conversion is 2.5 to 3.5 square feet of wall and ceiling surface per square foot of floor space, depending on ceiling height and room layout. Labor is the dominant cost, typically 70% to 85% of the total, so quotes vary far more with local labor rates and prep requirements than with the paint itself. Prep work is where budgets move: a room with clean, sound drywall can go straight to two coats, while walls with cracks, water stains, or old textured finishes may need hours of patching and priming before a can is opened. Changing from a dark color to a light one usually adds a coat. Expect painters to quote lower for empty rooms and new construction, and higher for furnished spaces that need masking and moving. Getting three written quotes that itemize prep, coats, and included surfaces (walls, ceilings, trim, doors) is the reliable way to compare bids on equal terms.

FAQ

How much does it cost to paint one room?

A typical room costs $350 to $850 to paint professionally, depending on size, prep work, and whether ceilings and trim are included.

Is it cheaper to paint yourself?

DIY materials for a room run $100 to $300, so you save the labor, which is 70% to 85% of a professional quote. The tradeoff is time (a weekend per room) and finish quality on prep and cutting in.

How long does interior paint last?

A quality interior paint job lasts 5 to 10 years in most rooms; hallways, kitchens, and kids' rooms often need refreshing sooner.
