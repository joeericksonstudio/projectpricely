Bathroom Remodel prices in Arizona

Arizona labor costs track close to the national average, with Phoenix metro demand pushing prices slightly above the state baseline during the peak fall-to-spring building season. Summer heat compresses outdoor work schedules, which can add labor hours to exterior projects. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.02x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside Arizona can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • Scope (cosmetic refresh vs gut renovation)
  • Moving plumbing (relocating a toilet or shower is the costliest change)
  • Tile choice and coverage area
  • Shower vs tub (walk-in shower conversions run $3,000 to $15,000)
  • Fixture and vanity quality
  • Water damage or mold discovered during demo

What to know before you get quotes

Bathroom remodels have the widest per-square-foot range of any room because bathrooms pack expensive trades - plumbing, tile, waterproofing - into the smallest spaces. Expect $100 to $150 per square foot for a minor renovation, $150 to $300 for a partial remodel, and $250 to $500 for a full remodel with layout changes. Size drives total cost in a predictable way: a 5-by-7-foot hall bath full remodel typically lands at $9,000 to $17,500, while a 100-square-foot primary bath with a walk-in shower and double vanity commonly runs $20,000 to $40,000. The decisions that move the budget most are keeping plumbing in place (moving a toilet flange alone can cost $2,500 or more), the tile selection and how much wall area it covers, and whether the shower is prefab or custom-tiled. Labor dominates at 40% to 65% of the total because bathrooms are trade-dense. Always get an itemized bid that separates demo, plumbing, tile, fixtures, and finishes, and hold a 10% to 15% contingency - water damage behind old tile is the rule, not the exception.

FAQ

What is the cheapest way to remodel a bathroom?

Keep the layout and plumbing in place, refinish rather than replace the tub, use a prefab shower unit, and choose mid-range fixtures. A minor renovation of a small bath can stay under $5,000.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A cosmetic refresh takes 1 to 2 weeks; a full remodel runs 3 to 6 weeks, longer if tile or glass is custom-ordered.

Does a bathroom remodel add home value?

Midrange bathroom remodels typically recoup 60% to 70% of their cost at resale, and an added or modernized bath often helps a home sell faster.
