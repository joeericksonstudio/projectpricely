Landscaping prices in Arizona

Arizona labor costs track close to the national average, with Phoenix metro demand pushing prices slightly above the state baseline during the peak fall-to-spring building season. Summer heat compresses outdoor work schedules, which can add labor hours to exterior projects. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.02x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside Arizona can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • Yard size and terrain (slopes and poor access cost more)
  • Scope (softscape only vs hardscape like patios, walkways, retaining walls)
  • Plant selection and mature sizes
  • Grading, drainage, and irrigation work
  • Existing landscape removal and site prep

What to know before you get quotes

Landscaping is priced per square foot for installation work, typically $4 to $12 depending on complexity, while crews charge $50 to $100 per hour for a two-person team on smaller jobs. Labor dominates the budget at around 80% of the total, which is why designs heavy on grading, retaining walls, and stonework cost far more than planting-heavy plans on flat ground. A complete landscape installation for a new home averages $3,000 to $16,000, and realtors commonly suggest budgeting 5% to 15% of the home's value for a full design-build. Individual features carry their own price lists: sod runs $1 to $2 per square foot, sprinkler systems $1,800 to $5,200, and artificial turf $6 to $20 per square foot. The cheapest way to control cost is to phase the project, doing grading and irrigation first and adding plantings over seasons. Quotes should itemize demolition, grading, materials by type and quantity, and labor, because lump-sum landscaping bids are notoriously hard to compare.

FAQ

How much does it cost to landscape a yard?

Most homeowners pay $4 to $12 per square foot for professional landscaping installation, or $3,000 to $16,000 for a complete new landscape on an average lot.

How much do landscapers charge per hour?

Landscaping crews charge $50 to $100 per hour for a 2-person crew, or $25 to $50 per person, for maintenance and small projects.

Does landscaping add value to a home?

Well-executed landscaping typically recoups much of its cost at resale; realtors often recommend investing 5% to 15% of the home's value, with curb appeal projects returning the most.
