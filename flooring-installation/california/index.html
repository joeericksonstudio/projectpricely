Flooring Installation prices in California

California labor rates run well above the national average, especially in the Bay Area, Los Angeles, and San Diego metro areas. Contractor licensing (CSLB) and permitting are stricter than most states, which adds soft costs to most quotes. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.28x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside California can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • Material type (carpet and laminate at $2 to $8 installed; hardwood $11 to $25; marble $10 to $40)
  • Subfloor condition and leveling or repair work
  • Removal and disposal of existing flooring
  • Room shape, closets, stairs, and transitions (cutting and waste)
  • Moving furniture and replacing baseboards or trim

What to know before you get quotes

Flooring is quoted per square foot installed, and material selection swings the price more than anything else: carpet and laminate start near $2 to $8 per square foot installed, luxury vinyl plank runs $4 to $16, and solid hardwood reaches $11 to $25, with marble and premium tile above that. Labor alone is $2 to $8 per square foot, so on budget materials the product and the labor cost about the same, while on premium materials the product dominates. The line items that surprise homeowners are the ones around the edges: removing old flooring, leveling a wavy subfloor, moving furniture, and replacing baseboards can each add meaningful cost, and stairs are priced per step rather than per square foot. Buy 5% to 10% extra material for cuts and waste (more for diagonal or herringbone layouts), and confirm whether the quote includes underlayment, transitions, and disposal. Written quotes that itemize material grade, square footage, prep, and removal make competing bids comparable.

FAQ

What is the cheapest flooring to install?

Carpet and basic laminate are the cheapest at roughly $2 to $8 per square foot installed. Sheet vinyl and builder-grade LVP are close behind.

How much does hardwood floor installation cost?

Solid hardwood runs $11 to $25 per square foot installed; engineered wood is $7 to $20. A 1,000 sq ft hardwood project typically totals $11,000 to $25,000.

How long does flooring installation take?

A pro crew typically installs 500 to 1,000 sq ft of laminate or LVP per day. Hardwood and tile take longer, and hardwood often needs days of acclimation before installation.
