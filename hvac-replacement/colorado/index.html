HVAC Replacement prices in Colorado

Colorado labor costs run slightly above the national average, driven by sustained Front Range growth - Denver, Boulder, and Colorado Springs have kept contractor demand ahead of supply for years. Mountain weather compresses the exterior work season, and hail exposure keeps roofing and exterior trades especially busy. Our estimate applies a regional adjustment of 1.07x the national average rate. Treat this as a planning figure - metro areas inside Colorado can sit well above or below the state-level adjustment, and only written quotes from licensed local contractors settle a real price.

What drives the price

  • System size in tons or BTUs (sized to the home, typically 1 ton per 500 to 600 sq ft)
  • Efficiency rating (high-SEER units cost more up front, less to run)
  • Ductwork condition (replacement adds $2,000 to $5,000)
  • Fuel type (gas, electric, oil, heat pump) and brand tier
  • Permits, old equipment disposal, and add-ons like zoning or smart thermostats

What to know before you get quotes

HVAC replacement is usually priced two ways: per square foot of home ($3 to $6 installed) or as a flat change-out for the furnace and air conditioner ($5,000 to $11,000 on average). The equipment itself is the bulk of the bill at $4,000 to $8,000, with installation labor of $1,000 to $3,000 on top. Three scope levels explain most quote spread: a change-out reuses existing ductwork and takes about a day; an installation with new ductwork runs $7,000 to $16,000 over three to five days; and add-ons like zoning, humidifiers, or air purifiers push totals to $9,500 to $18,500. Ductwork lasts 10 to 20 years, so systems older than 15 years often need duct repairs with the new equipment. Efficiency ratings matter for the long term: high-SEER air conditioners and high-efficiency furnaces cost more up front but cut monthly energy bills for the life of the system. A common rule of thumb: if the repair cost multiplied by the system's age exceeds $5,000, replacement beats repair. Always get itemized quotes naming the equipment model, efficiency rating, warranty, and whether ductwork and permits are included.

FAQ

How much does a new HVAC system cost for a 2,000 sq ft house?

A full HVAC system for a 2,000 sq ft home costs $6,000 to $12,000 installed, depending on efficiency tier and ductwork condition.

Should I repair or replace my HVAC system?

If the repair cost multiplied by the system's age in years exceeds $5,000, or the system is over 10 to 15 years old with rising energy bills, replacement is usually the better value.

How long do HVAC systems last?

Furnaces last 15 to 20 years and air conditioners 10 to 15 years with regular maintenance. Ductwork lasts 10 to 20 years before needing repair or replacement.
